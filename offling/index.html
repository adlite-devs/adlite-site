Offling

Screen time limits with a tiny friend to raise. Stay within your limits, and your Offling hatches, grows and evolves.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Limits that fit you

Set limits for apps, websites and reels. Choose gentle limits with a pause, or strict ones that stay locked.

Hearts for a little extra

Out of time? Spend a heart to keep going. You choose how many you get each day.

Earn play time

Time in reading or learning apps, and finishing your daily tasks, earns play time for games.

Raise an Offling

Good days earn stars. Eggs hatch, Offlings evolve, and special tasks unlock special forms.

A console on your home screen

The pocket console widget shows the time, your limits and your Offling at a glance.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Backup to your own Google Drive is optional.
