Offling Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Offling ("the app") is published by Adlite.org ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is needed, and the choices you have. In short: Offling works on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, there are no accounts, and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Information the app processes on your device

To work, Offling uses the following information. It is processed and stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

  • App usage: which apps you open and for how long, read through Android's Usage Access, to show your screen-time statistics, apply your limits and award play time.
  • Installed apps: the list of apps on your phone, so you can choose which ones to limit, use as target apps or use as reward apps.
  • Screen monitor (optional): if you turn on Offling's accessibility service, it counts short videos (reels and shorts) in the apps you choose, and checks the address shown in your browser against your list of limited websites. To tell a reels player from the rest of an app, it looks at the names of the app's screens and views, and, in apps that hide those names (such as Facebook and Snapchat), at the labels of the buttons you tap, for words like "Reels". Addresses and labels are compared on the device and are never saved, logged or sent anywhere. It does not read messages, passwords or anything you type.
  • What you create in the app: your limits, tasks, journal entries, Offlings and settings.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Usage access: to show your screen-time statistics and apply your limits.
  • Display over other apps: to show the mindful pause or a lock screen on top of an app.
  • Notifications (recommended): to show that the limits are running, to tell you when your egg is ready to hatch, and to warn you if your limits stop working, for example when Android closes the monitor.
  • Unrestricted battery use (optional): to stop Android from switching the limits off.
  • Accessibility service (optional): the screen monitor described above.
  • Internet: used only for the optional Google Drive backup.

Offling does not use these permissions for any other purpose.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store your personal information. The app has no account system and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code. The information described above stays on your device, except for the optional backup described in section 4, which goes to your own Google account.

4. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Continue with Google, Offling signs in with your Google account and saves a backup of your app data (your limits, tasks, journal, Offlings and settings) in the app's private folder in your own Google Drive. Offling asks for access to that folder only, so it cannot see your other files, and we cannot see your backup. The backup is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

The backup also holds a random ID made for this installation of Offling, so that the app can tell when another phone has saved over your backup and ask before replacing either. It identifies the installation, not you, is kept on your phone and in that backup only, and is never sent anywhere else.

You can stop backing up at any time in Settings. To delete the backup, choose Delete the backup in Settings, or open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Offling and choose Delete hidden app data.

5. Purchases

Offling Basic and Offling Premium are one-time purchases made through Google Play. Google handles the payment, so we never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app which plan you have bought, and the app keeps that on your phone to unlock it. Purchases are covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. If you choose to share a picture of an Offling, it goes only to the app you pick in Android's share menu.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Delete all data in Settings, uninstalling Offling, or clearing its storage in Android's settings deletes everything the app stored on your phone, including what its limits keep while the app is closed.

Offling switches off Android's own backup and phone-to-phone transfer for its data, so nothing is copied to your Google account or to a new phone except the backup you choose in section 4. A Google Drive backup stays in your Google account until you delete it as described there.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The Google Drive backup is sent over an encrypted connection. To protect you from altered copies, the app checks on your phone that it is the official version of Offling; nothing is sent for this check.

9. Children

Offling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your choices and rights

You stay in control of your information: you can change or delete it in the app, turn off any optional permission in Android's settings, and delete your backup at any time. Because we do not hold your personal information, there is nothing stored on our side to access, correct or delete. For any question or request, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top, and if the changes are significant we will let you know in the app.
